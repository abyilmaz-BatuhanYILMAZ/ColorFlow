# Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası
**Color Flow Adventure** — Android package: `com.colorflowadventure.puzzle`  
Effective date / Yürürlük tarihi: 31 October / Ekim 2026

## English

### Developer and contact
Color Flow Adventure is provided by Abdullah Batuhan YILMAZ. For privacy questions or requests, contact **abdullahbatuhanyilmaz@gmail.com**.

### Game data stored on your device
The game does not require an account. Progress, unlocked levels, rewards and language preferences are stored locally on your device. The developer does not operate a server that receives this gameplay data. You can remove local data through Android Settings → Apps → Color Flow Adventure → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

### Advertising and third-party processing
The app displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to manage applicable advertising consent.

Google's advertising services may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance data. These data support advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Ad processing may occur even when ads are not personalized; the data used depend on consent, region, device settings and Google's service configuration. The developer may receive aggregated advertising reports through AdMob.

The app does not request access to contacts, microphone, camera or precise GPS location. It does not ask you to enter a name, email address or payment information.

See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Choices and consent
Where required, Google displays a consent form. Where Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, the app displays “Reklam tercihleri / Ad choices” so you can revisit your choices. You can also use Android's advertising privacy controls to reset or delete your advertising ID. Puzzle gameplay is available offline; advertisements require internet access.

### Retention, deletion and security
Local game data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Google controls retention of advertising data under its policies; the developer does not set a universal retention period for Google's data. Advertising SDK transmissions use encrypted connections. No electronic system is guaranteed completely secure.

Contact the developer for questions or applicable access, correction or deletion requests. Data held by Google may require using Google's privacy controls or contacting Google directly.

### Children
The app does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact the address above so the matter can be investigated and appropriate deletion steps taken.

### Changes
Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Uygulama, Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) ile banner reklam gösterir. İlgili reklam izinlerini yönetmek için Google User Messaging Platform kullanılır.

Google reklam hizmetleri; IP adresi (yaklaşık konum hakkında bilgi verebilir), reklam kimliği ve diğer cihaz tanımlayıcıları, uygulama/reklam etkileşimleri ve tanılama/performans verilerini toplayabilir ve paylaşabilir. Bu veriler reklam sunumu, ölçüm, analiz ve sahtekârlığın önlenmesi için kullanılır. Kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamlarda da veri işlenebilir; kapsam izinlere, bölgeye, cihaz ayarlarına ve Google hizmet yapılandırmasına bağlıdır. Geliştirici AdMob üzerinden toplulaştırılmış reklam raporları görebilir.

Uygulama rehbere, mikrofona, kameraya veya hassas GPS konumuna erişim istemez. Sizden ad, e-posta veya ödeme bilgisi girmeniz istenmez.

Ayrıntılar: [Google Gizlilik Politikası](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=tr) ve [Google'ın uygulamalardan aldığı bilgileri kullanması](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=tr).
